# saveflight
saveflight is our solution for the turbulance caused by the wind turbines for birds.
problem: Wind turbines are essential for clean energy, but their fast-spinning blades are a serious hazard to birds and bats. Blade tips move so quickly that they blur into what looks like empty space, so wildlife flies straight into them. Large birds also carry too much momentum to turn at the last second, and silent nocturnal hunters like owls give off no sound for acoustic monitors to pick up. Systems that can detect and respond to wildlife, such as radar and thermal imaging, are expensive, and most turbine operators, especially in developing regions and community-owned projects, can’t afford them. As a result, most turbines have no active wildlife protection at all.

The core problem in one line

There is no low-cost, real-time system that detects an approaching bird and responds before a collision.

Who is affected

Birds and bats, especially migratory and nocturnal species.
Small wind operators, who can’t afford industrial monitoring systems.
Conservation and regulatory bodies, who need collision data they don’t currently get.

The three gaps SafeFlight addresses

Cost: existing detection systems are priced far beyond small operators, while SafeFlight is built from low-cost parts.
Silent and night-flying species: distance sensing doesn’t depend on a bird making noise, and the light sensor widens the detection zone in the dark.
Reaction time: reading the sensors at the edge, on the board itself, means a response within about 200 ms instead of waiting for a person to review logs.

solution:When something enters the set perimeter, the LED lights, the relay triggers a deterrent (buzzer), and the LCD shows the alert.
